Hi, I am Edoardo, but feel free to call me "Edo". 

I am researcher in *eXplainable AI* (XAI) and *Natural Language Processing* (NLP) with a background in mathematics and experience in the industry, academia, and start-ups. 

I hold a PhD in Computer Science from the Technical University of Munich under the supervision of [Prof. Dr. Georg Groh](https://socvm1.cit.tum.de/persons/georg-groh/) at the [Social Computing Group](https://socvm1.cit.tum.de/persons/). My dissertation ***"Explainable AI for the Human-Centric Development of NLP Models"*** has a strong focus on post-hoc explainability techniques to improve LLMs' interpretability, robustness, and controllability. I co-authored 10+ scientific publications, mostly as leading author, and led over 20 ML-based project.

Feel free to reach out if you have a proposal or any idea you'd like to collaborate on. You can also access [my publications](./publications) and my [resume](./cv).
