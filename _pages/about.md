Hi, I am Edoardo, but if can call me "Edo". 

I am researcher in *eXplainable AI* (XAI) and *Natural Language Processing* (NLP) with experience in the industry, academia, and start-ups. I hold a PhD in Computer Science from the Technical University of Munich under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Georg Groh at the Social Computing Group. My research has a strong focus on post-hoc explainability techniques to improve the interpretability, robustness, and controllability of deployed NLP models. I co-authored 10+ scientific publications, mostly as leading author, and I led over 20 ML-based project.
